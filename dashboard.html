🎓 Course Presentation & Viva Q&A Cheat Sheet

Demand prediction is a forecasting problem (predicting macroscopic applicant volume over semesters), whereas room allocation is an assignment optimization problem (pairing individual students to specific beds under strict constraints). Keeping them modular allows retraining the ML model without altering allocation constraints.

Greedy allocation gives early applicants their preferred rooms but severely penalizes later applicants, leading to high inequality. The Hungarian algorithm solves the Linear Sum Assignment Problem globally, maximizing total cohort welfare and achieving a superior Gini fairness coefficient.

All allocations are evaluated using a hidden ground-truth satisfaction function, rather than the model's own predicted scores. This ensures the benchmark measures real student happiness objectively.
